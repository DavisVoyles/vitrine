      In the headset

      • Turn your left palm toward you for the menu: scans, look, Auto, put back, video and Exit VR. Poke a button with your right index finger, or point at it and pinch or pull the trigger.
      • Grip, trigger or pinch on the scan to pick it up; it moves and turns with your hand. Both hands: pull apart or together to scale.
      • In a space, pinch or grip anywhere and pull to move through it; with both hands, turn and scale it. Left stick walks, right stick snap-turns.
      • Trigger or pinch on the video to play or pause; grip it to move it.
      • Right A next scan · B colour or hologram · stick click Auto · Left X put it back · Y video
      • To leave without the menu: the Meta button, or turn your right palm toward you and pinch.

      Keys on this computer

      • ← → previous / next scan
      • H colour or hologram · A Auto
      • R put it back · 1 real size · + − scale
      • V or Space play or pause the video
